01 / The signal

Every discharge is a handoff. More of them now carry an ask.

The rise is not just in how often requests appear. The work being passed across the hospital-primary care boundary is changing shape too.

03 / Reading the letters

From free text to a measurable ask.

Discharge summaries are written for people, not spreadsheets. We prompted a large language model to identify the requests inside them, then grouped each request by type and timeframe.

+ This makes a familiar clinical handoff visible at scale without reducing it to a single count.

04 / Why it matters

Discharge is not the end of care. It is where the next task begins.

Rising request volumes and longer timeframes point to growing post-discharge complexity and system strain. More informational messaging may add to GP workload without matching resources.

The takeawayAlign commissioning and discharge policy with the work that follows.
